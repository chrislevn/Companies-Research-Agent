Connect your email

The agent only ever reads your mail. It never sends, deletes or changes anything.

Works with Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, Zoho, Fastmail and company mail servers. Takes about two minutes.

      Google will say the app is not verified

      That is expected: the app is the one you just created, it runs on this computer, and only you use it — so Google has nothing to verify it against. Reading your own mail with your own credentials is exactly what it is for.
